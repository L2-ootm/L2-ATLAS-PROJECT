# Building the list as an international applicant

**Use when:** shaping, culling or reporting on the college list.

## No chancing. Ever.

Do not estimate admission probability as a number, a band, or a phrase that
implies one. See [doctrine](doctrine.md) for why: headline admit rates are the
wrong denominator for an F-1 applicant needing substantial aid, and need-aware
review changes the question entirely.

If asked directly for chances, say what is actually knowable and offer that: the
school's policy for internationals, whether it meets full need for them, how
complete its materials are, and how many days remain.

## The two facts that shape this list

For each school, held in the record with provenance:

- **need_aware_intl** — whether the school is need-aware *for internationals
  specifically*. This is usually a different and harsher policy than the headline
  domestic one, and that distinction is the entire reason it is stored.
- **meets_full_need_intl** — whether demonstrated need is met in full for
  internationals.

Both are three-state: true, false, or unknown. **Unknown must never be reported
as false.** A list built on an assumption nobody made deliberately is worse than
a shorter list built on checked facts.

## Culling

The list is culled toward roughly twenty schools, with exactly one restrictive
early plan. When asked to help cut:

- Cut on aid policy first. A school that is need-aware for internationals and
  does not meet full need is a school whose acceptance may be unusable.
- Then cut on unverified facts — a school nobody has checked is a school nobody
  has decided about.
- Never cut the last affordable option to make room for a more selective one.

Report what to cut and why, and write the reasoning back to the record rather
than leaving it only in the reply.
