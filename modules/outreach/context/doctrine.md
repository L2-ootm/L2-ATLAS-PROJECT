# Outreach doctrine

Outbound at L2 is **evidence-gated**, not volume-gated. The unit of progress is
a verified fact about a prospect, not a message sent.

## The rule that decides everything

No message goes out until you can name, from evidence: (1) the prospect's
current offer, (2) a specific gap in it, (3) why that gap costs them something
now, (4) why L2 is a credible party to close it, (5) the smallest test that
would prove or kill the thesis, and (6) the one-line ask. Six unanswered
questions means the prospect is still in research. Write the answers into the
record; if you cannot, say so and stop.

## Stages

`research → ready → sent → replied → discovery → validation → pilot → nurture |
closed`. Movement is earned by evidence, never by elapsed time. `nurture` is an
honest hold, not a soft failure — a prospect whose hypothesis was invalidated
goes to nurture with the invalidation recorded, so nobody re-pitches it.

## Evidence classes

Every signal carries one: `verified` (a source you actually read),
`reported` (someone said it, unverified), `hypothesis` (your inference).
A `hypothesis` may never be stated to a prospect as fact, and a gate cannot
pass on hypothesis alone. Record the source URL with the signal.

## The strongest thesis kills itself first

Before proposing anything, check whether they already have it. A pitch for
something the prospect already sells is the single most expensive mistake in
this pipeline: it burns the relationship and marks L2 as someone who did not
look. Search their site, their store, their pinned posts. If the thing exists,
record the invalidation and move on.

## Message craft

The first message exists to earn a reply, nothing else. One specific
observation, one question, no attachments, no dossier, no percentages, no
pitch. If the draft has a second paragraph, it is not the first message.

## What the agent does and does not do

Do: research, verify, score, draft, schedule, record, follow up on the ledger,
report what is due and what is stalled. Do not: send, promise, price, or claim
a result you have not verified. Sending is a human act (see `compliance`).

## Record as you go

Every meaningful step ends with a write: a `signal` for a new fact, a `touch`
for any contact, a `gate` for any decision, an update to the prospect's stage
and `next_action`. Work that is not recorded did not happen — the next run
starts from the records, not from this conversation.
